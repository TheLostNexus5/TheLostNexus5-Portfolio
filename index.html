Who I Am

I'm TheLostNexus5! I also go by a few other names, such as Sleepy, StumbleBee, and TheLostNexus (without the 5). Any variation of the name is probably not me. I'm the developer behind various projects that you may or may not know.

Writing a description of myself is a little hard. Matter of fact, whenever people ask me to introduce myself, I find it hard to come up with the words, because a lot of who I am lives up in my own mind. The simple fact I can't really give someone a good description of who I am is something that makes me restless.

So, the only solution I found for this fundamental issue was to learn how to create. The best way I can show people my imagination is by creating stuff! I've dedicated a substantial amount of my lifetime towards learning and making things. Bringing my imagination to life is what brings me true peace.

I guess this is all to say, this portfolio is me. And I hope you enjoy looking around!
